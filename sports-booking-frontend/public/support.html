Common Questions

How do I book a turf?
Open the app, choose a ground, pick a date and slot, then confirm your booking.

How do I split costs?
When a game is completed, the moderator selects who played and the app calculates the per-person amount.

How do I reset my password?
Go to Profile → Change Password after logging in with your temporary password.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm. Your account will be scheduled for deletion and personal data will be purged after 90 days.
