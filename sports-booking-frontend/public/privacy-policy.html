Privacy Policy for Elite Turf Booking

Effective date: August 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Elite Turf Booking ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve the app:

  • Account information: name, phone number, and optional email address.
  • Game and booking data: game schedules, player confirmations, ground bookings, and cost-split records.
  • Device permissions: camera and photo library access if you choose to upload media in discussions.
  • Usage data: app interactions and crash logs to help us improve stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Allow moderators to organize games, split costs, and track payments.
  • Send booking reminders and game updates via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Improve app performance and fix issues.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with other app users as needed for group game management (for example, showing player names to confirmed participants). We may use trusted service providers for hosting and app distribution.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), to protect your data. Passwords are stored securely using hashing.

6. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You can update your profile information, change your password, and delete your account directly inside the app:

  • In-app deletion: go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm.
  • Public deletion page: you can also request deletion by visiting https://ground-booking-eleg.onrender.com/#delete-account and entering your phone number and password.

When you delete your account, it is scheduled for deletion. Your personal data is retained for a 90-day grace period so you can restore your account by logging in again. After 90 days, your phone number, email, password hash, and other personal identifiers are permanently replaced with anonymous values, and your game participation, payment, vote, photo, and notification records are removed.

7. Children's Privacy

Elite Turf Booking is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or by email.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to request deletion of your data, contact us at eliteaiwin@gmail.com.
